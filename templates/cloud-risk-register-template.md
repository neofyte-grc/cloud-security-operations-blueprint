# Cloud Risk Register Template

**Organization:** Peachtree Logistics Group (PLG), fictional  
**Purpose:** Record, assess, assign, and revisit risks affecting the proposed dispatch workload.

Copy the blank record below for each risk. The project scenarios R-01 through R-08 are described in `docs/05-cloud-risk-assessment.md`.

## Scoring guide

- **Likelihood:** 1 = Rare; 2 = Unlikely; 3 = Possible; 4 = Likely; 5 = Very likely.
- **Impact:** 1 = Minimal; 2 = Minor; 3 = Moderate; 4 = Major; 5 = Severe.
- **Initial score:** Likelihood × Impact.
- **Priority:** 1–5 Low; 6–12 Moderate; 13–25 High.

Residual ratings are provisional until the proposed controls have been implemented and tested.

## Review rules

Review High risks at least quarterly and after a material incident, architecture change, new data category, failed control test, or missed recovery objective. Record the change in rating and its evidence. Do not reduce a risk score solely because a control has been documented.
