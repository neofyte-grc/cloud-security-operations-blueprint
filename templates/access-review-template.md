## Completion check

- [ ] The exported population matches the defined review scope.
- [ ] Every identity has a recorded decision and reviewer.
- [ ] Unexpected privileged access was escalated.
- [ ] Modify/remove decisions were executed and verified.
- [ ] Overdue actions have an owner and due date.
- [ ] The reviewer signed off on the final record.
